2026 · Journal article

Policy Mix in Japan's EV Battery Industry

Japanese firms' share of the global battery market fell from 51.7% to 7.6% between 2015 and 2023 while Chinese firms rose from 27.4% to 64.1%. The paper reads Japan's response as a policy mix — four instruments introduced after the Economic Security Promotion Act — and asks which combinations cohere.

2026 · Patent, filing in preparation

Separating AI Compute Power to Diagnose Operating Health

Data centres know their total electricity draw but not how much of it AI workloads actually consume. The invention isolates the AI-attributed component, uses it to diagnose an organization's operating state, and controls server and cooling resources when anomalies appear.

Tool · Web app

Portfolio Monte Carlo Simulation

A browser-only simulator for asset allocation, contribution and withdrawal plans. It runs thousands of paths to show the distribution of outcomes and the probability of reaching a target. Nothing entered leaves the browser.

2026 · Pilot study

Early-Warning Indicators for SME Distress

Twenty indicators across four families — news sentiment, macroeconomic conditions, financial position, and credit markets — assembled into a dashboard. Topic modelling over 762 collected articles tracks how the language of distress shifts before the numbers do.

2025 · Patent analysis

Patent Atlas: Semiconductor Etching

8,993 granted US patents projected with TF-IDF and t-SNE onto a two-dimensional map of 16 technology clusters, with density terrain, applicant competition structure, and an IPC convergence network.

2026 · Patent Universiade entry

Tactile Sensing Patent Strategy

2,786 tactile sensor patents across five jurisdictions. The analysis finds ETRI's portfolio inverted — thick at output and rendering, thin at the sensing layer it depends on — and proposes an Anchor–Connect–Expand response.
